Terms

1 October 2026

This landing page explains Classify Junction and links to the marketplace. DigiBillMate built the software. Using the marketplace means the points below.

Browsing

You can read published advertisements with no account. Only advertisements the marketplace has published are shown. Search filters are part of the page address.

Advertisements you post

You are responsible for what you submit. Advertisements are reviewed by the team before they are published, usually within 2 working days. Inappropriate items that may hurt religious, cultural, or social sentiments must be avoided.

A description can be up to 400 characters. You can submit up to 20 advertisements a day. Editing after you submit is turned off, so check the title, category, audience, and description first. If an advertisement is rejected, My Advertisements shows a short reason.

The category you choose stays on the advertisement, because the posting fee belongs to that category.

Fees

Each category has a posting price and a separate price to view a mobile number. A price of ₹0 is free. On 1 October 2026, Sell costs ₹100 to post and Share, Buy, and Others are free to post. Viewing a mobile number is ₹50 in every category. The marketplace shows the current amount before you pay, and that shown amount is the one that applies to the payment you start.

A free category goes to review when you submit. A paid category goes to review after the payment is verified. Viewing a number you have already viewed is not charged again.

Mobile numbers

The number on your profile is what other people can ask to view. Public pages show it masked. Do not post a number you do not want used as the contact on your advertisement.

Reports

Report a listing that is fake or a scam, incorrect, no longer available, inappropriate, a duplicate, or something else. One report per person per advertisement. You cannot report your own. The team reviews open reports and may dismiss the report, close the advertisement, or remove it.

Staff

The staff sign-in is for employees and administrators only. Customers use Google.

This page

Prices and rules on this landing page describe the marketplace as it stood on 1 October 2026. If the marketplace and this page ever disagree, the marketplace is the one you are using.
