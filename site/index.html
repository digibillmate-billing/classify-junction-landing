If you are looking

  • Open the marketplace. No account.
  • Choose a category: Sell, Share, Buy, or Others.
  • Choose who it is for: Chennai, Delhi, Kolkata, or Location Independent.
  • Open a listing. The mobile number is masked.
  • Report a listing that is fake, wrong, finished, inappropriate, or a duplicate.

If you are posting

  • Write the title and a description of up to 400 characters.
  • Pick the category. The posting fee, if there is one, is shown before you pay.
  • Pick the audience, then submit. Google sign-in starts here if you are signed out.
  • A free post goes straight to review. A paid post goes to review after the payment is verified.
  • Follow the status under My Advertisements, including a short reason if it is rejected.

Questions

Before you open the board.

How do I download the app?

Open the marketplace and use Download app. On a computer, Chrome or Edge installs it from that button. On an iPhone, open the marketplace in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. On Android, use Download app or Install app in the Chrome menu. The icon then opens Classify Junction like any other app.

Do I need an account to look?

No. Browsing, search, and opening a listing are public. The marketplace asks for Google only when you post, report a listing, open your own advertisements, or view a contact number.

How does sign-in work?

With Google. The first time, Classify Junction asks for your name and a 10-digit Indian mobile number. The email from Google is your identity, and it is not edited. A +91 or a leading 0 on the mobile number is accepted.

When does my advertisement go public?

After the team reviews it, usually within 2 working days. A free post goes to that review when you submit. A paid post shows its fee first; after the payment is verified, it joins the same review. Nothing is public before it is published.

Can I change an advertisement after I submit it?

Not at the moment. Read the title, category, audience, and description before you send it. The category stays with the advertisement, because the fee belongs to the category you chose. If it is rejected, My Advertisements shows a short reason.

Why is the phone number hidden?

So it is not copied off the page by everyone who can browse. You see a mask, for example 98765 4XXXX. The full number is returned only when you are allowed to see it. Viewing a number you have already viewed is not charged again.

What if a listing is fake or offensive?

Use Report on the listing. The reasons are fake or scam, incorrect information, no longer available, inappropriate, duplicate, or other (other asks for a short detail). One report per person per advertisement. The team can dismiss the report, close the advertisement, or remove it. You cannot report your own advertisement.
