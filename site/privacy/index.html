This page covers two places. This landing page only describes the service. The marketplace at classify-junction.digibillmate-billing.workers.dev is where advertisements, sign-in, and payments happen. DigiBillMate built both.

This landing page

The landing page is a static site. It does not ask you to sign in, it does not run payments, and it does not use analytics or set its own cookies. The host, Cloudflare, may keep ordinary request logs, such as IP address, browser, and the page requested, the way any public website does.

The marketplace

Browsing does not need an account. When you sign in, it is with Google. The first time, the marketplace asks for your name and a 10-digit Indian mobile number. The Google email is your identity and is not edited on the profile.

From that point the marketplace keeps:

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, and Google email.
  • Advertisements you submit, including title, category, audience, description, status, and a rejection reason if one is given.
  • A record when you view a mobile number.
  • Payment records for a posting fee or a mobile-number view: the amount, the type, the status, the time, and the gateway reference. The amount is taken from the marketplace’s price, not typed in the browser.
  • Reports you file on a listing, including the reason.

Contact numbers

A public listing shows a masked mobile number, for example 98765 4XXXX. The full number is not included in the public page. It is returned to the person who posted the advertisement, or after a verified payment to view that number.

Staff

Employees and administrators sign in separately, with email and a password, at the staff sign-in. A Google customer sign-in cannot become a staff account. Staff actions on advertisements, prices, and reports are recorded so the team can see who did what.

Questions about a listing

Use Report on that listing in the marketplace. The marketplace has not published a separate contact email.
